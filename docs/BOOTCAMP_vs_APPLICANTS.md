# Scope Definition: Bootcampers vs Applicants

## Key Distinction

### **Applicants** (All 400+ from 2023-2025)
- Everyone who applied to Project Access
- Used for: Dashboard demographics, historical context
- Limited tracking (just what they wanted)

### **Bootcampers** (85-90 in 2026)
- Only those SELECTED & ATTENDED the bootcamp
- Used for: Three-stage survey pipeline (pre/mid/post)
- Full tracking through entire journey

## Pipeline Scope

### BOOTCAMPERS ONLY (Three-Stage Surveys)
- Survey 1 (Pre): Dream universities, target destinations, fields, motivations
- Survey 2 (Mid): Mind changes, progress, challenges, confidence updates
- Survey 3 (Post): Where got in, where going, bootcamp impact

### ALL APPLICANTS (Demographic Context)
- High school distribution
- Geographic distribution (cities)
- Field of study interests
- Destination preferences
- Used for: Comparison against bootcamper outcomes

## Important
- Do NOT mix applicant data with bootcamper survey data
- Bootcamper pipeline is SEPARATE from dashboard
- Can cross-reference but keep tables distinct
